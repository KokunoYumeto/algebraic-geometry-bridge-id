## Frozen source anomalies requiring visible reader treatment

The frozen authority remains unchanged. A later Indonesian reader should
correct or clarify the following with an explicit source/editor note and a
corrections-ledger entry, preserving both the source wording and the adopted
mathematical reading.

1. `Prägarbe/Homomorphismus/Definition`, page 100670, revision 1091418,
   SHA-1 `25b6caefa95fbc4e3d70e4832d8b145f1ef8e196`: for `U \subseteq V`, the
   displayed naturality square puts `F(U)` above `F(V)` and labels downward
   arrows `rho_{U,V}`. Contravariance requires `F(V)` and `G(V)` above,
   `F(U)` and `G(U)` below, with restriction maps `rho^F_{V,U}` and
   `rho^G_{V,U}`.
2. `Prägarbe/Unterprägarbe/Definition`, page 109063, revision 1091422,
   SHA-1 `02b66c6c21c80ca97e38baeb45bafdaba70ad814`: the definition states only
   `G(U) \subseteq F(U)` and omits compatibility of the restriction maps.
   The intended definition must also require that each restriction of `G` is
   the restriction of the corresponding map of `F`.
3. `Gerichtetes System/Topologischer Filter/Kolimes/Einführung/Textabschnitt`,
   page 111689, revision 1102983, SHA-1
   `cffff538f143a33b9de57f53f5b38ae8bf35e25b`: the sentence beginning
   "Die wichtigsten Filter sind für und ..." is grammatically corrupted.
   Its context supports the reading that the important examples here are the
   neighbourhood filters of a point, consisting of all open neighbourhoods
   of that fixed point.
4. `Prägarbe/Homomorphismus/Verknüpfung/Fakt`, page 100679, revision
   1088434, SHA-1 `be8a8bd2ff653f8b100675f6a0c1b7c4146cb732`, and its Exercise 3.17
   page 111590, revision 1083988, SHA-1
   `77a0ec93f04c8cdf0e03053bf1d79e921f5fa248`: both say `Prägraben`, an
   evident typo for `Prägarben`.
5. `Mannigfaltigkeit/Tangentialbündel/Halm/Gleich/Aufgabe`, page 111691,
   revision 845017, SHA-1 `924f865986857dd9f2a725493b386fd163da405e`:
   Exercise 3.15 speaks of the "stalk of the tangent bundle". A stalk belongs
   to a presheaf; the surrounding unit makes clear that the intended object is
   the stalk of the presheaf of continuous sections of the tangent bundle.
