Von ihm ist der Kurs mit allen seinen Unterseiten (Vorlesungen, Arbeitsblätter) einschließlich der eingelesenen Seiten geschrieben und unter die freie Lizenz CC-by-sa 4.0 gestellt worden. This course was or is given by Holger Brenner (alias Benutzer:Bocardodarapti) at the University of Osnabrück. He is the author of this including all subpages (lectures, work sheets) and the included pages. He has put these materials under the free license CC-by-sa 4.0. Some minor corrections have been done by some students or assistents. The images are from Commons and their licenses are listet (in Wikiversity via their link, in the Pdf-versions explicitly in an appendix). The secretary (Benutzer:Mgausmann and the bot Benutzer:Arbota) establish the frame for the course following Benutzer:Arbota/Tätigkeiten/Kursaufbau, starts with copies of courses previously held by Holger Brenner, creates reference numbers and produces the Pdf-versions on the basis of automatically generated latex versions on Wikiversity.
