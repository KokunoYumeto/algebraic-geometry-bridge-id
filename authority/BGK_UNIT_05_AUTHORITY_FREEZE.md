## Frozen source anomalies and scope clarifications

The authority bytes remain unchanged. The Indonesian reader must visibly
disclose or ledger the following high-confidence repairs.

1. `AGC-BGK-U05-SRC-001`: `Prägarbe/Vergarbung/Definition` (page 100623,
   revision 1091423, SHA-1
   `06739a4accdc43b7cd6b4867f832ef77c27a5b9b`) quantifies `P in V subseteq U`
   without explicitly saying that `V` is open, although `F(V)` and the local
   neighbourhood role require it. Render `V` as an open neighbourhood and
   disclose the omitted qualifier.
2. `AGC-BGK-U05-SRC-002`: `Topologischer Raum/Matrix/Kerngarbe/Beispiel`
   (page 111991, revision 1085478, SHA-1
   `dff15617a57a4b91aa7d6d824b59f868ba5f8100`) writes
   `M:X -> Mat_{m x n}(C^0(X,R))` while describing a pointwise matrix-valued
   map. The type-correct alternatives are `M:X -> Mat_{m x n}(R)` or
   `M in Mat_{m x n}(C^0(X,R))`. Preserve the source formula and state the
   discrepancy.
3. `AGC-BGK-U05-SRC-003`: the same example writes
   `M:R^3 -> Mat_{1 x 3}(K)`, but `K` is undefined and the whole example is
   real. Preserve the source formula and identify `R` as the contextually
   intended scalar field.
4. `AGC-BGK-U05-SRC-004`: the proof on page 114556, revision 1112614,
   SHA-1 `b3a7ddd0f1211d0458d8411d3050553e5e9d96c4`, treats a section `g_i` as an
   element of a stalk `F_P`. The well-typed statement concerns the germ
   `(g_i)_P`. Use the germ and disclose the repair.
5. `AGC-BGK-U05-SRC-005`: the public Solution 5 says local sections
   `s_P in F(U_P)` “restrict to” the germ `t_P`. A section cannot restrict to
   a germ; its germ at `P` equals `t_P`. State the typed relation and disclose
   the clarification.
6. `AGC-BGK-U05-SRC-006`: `Prägarbe/Vergarbung/Fakt` (page 100658,
   revision 1102409, SHA-1
   `9d444c7d8d7256f5207a5a890a853acd231f18f2`) says `die natürliche
   Morphismus`; the evident grammatical form is `der natürliche Morphismus`.
   Translate normally and preserve the source-typo record.

The topological-group example uses arbitrary groups immediately after a
definition phrased for sheaves of commutative groups. Its construction is
valid without commutativity. The reader should label the example at the
general group-sheaf level and not silently claim that the source imposed
commutativity there. Literal equality notation for quotient stalks is retained
as the source's conventional identification; it is not counted as an error.
