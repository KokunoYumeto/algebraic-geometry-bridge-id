## Translation and mathematical-risk boundaries

1. Keep the lecture's standing premise that the defining polynomial has no
   repeated factors visibly attached to its derivative-based definitions of
   smoothness, singularity, and multiplicity. Do not present this elementary
   plane-curve criterion as scheme-theoretic smoothness over arbitrary or
   nonperfect fields.
2. Translate `Kartesisches Blatt` by the established mathematical name
   *folium Descartes*, not literally as a Cartesian sheet.
3. In Exercise 7, `(1,5)` is the coordinate pair `(1, 5)`, never the decimal
   number 1.5. Preserve the literal `\sqrt{1/3}` identity in Exercise 12's
   source title and formula.
4. Exercise 19 asks for three distinct cases: first partial derivative zero,
   second partial derivative zero, and both zero. Preserve all three.
5. Preserve exact semantic-title case and number, especially the plural title
   `Ebene algebraische Kurven/Einheitskreis...`; MediaWiki source titles must
   not be case-folded or normalized.
6. The smooth-connected-implies-irreducible corollary depends materially on
   Zariski connectedness and algebraic closure. Both hypotheses must remain
   visible.
7. Translation may add accessible descriptions and the component-credit
   reconciliation above. It must not invent missing exercise solutions or
   silently strengthen the source into a schemes-level theorem.

## Recorded source repairs and additive scope note

Production must expose, not silently absorb, the following bounded findings:

1. Exercise 8 is false as a geometric assertion over an arbitrary imperfect
   field when it assumes only squarefreeness in `K[X,Y]`; require the defining
   polynomial to be geometrically reduced. A perfect base field together with
   source squarefreeness is a sufficient special case.
2. Public Solution 10 prints only the left-hand expressions while calling
   them tangent-line equations. Restore `=0` in
   `2a(X-a)+2b(Y-b)=0`, equivalently `aX+bY-1=0`.
3. Exercise/Solution 16 omits its base field although the solution cancels
   factors 2 and 3 and factors with `sqrt(3)`. Bind the printed computation
   to `char(K) not in {2,3}` and `sqrt(3) in K`; in characteristic 2 the
   tangent cone is a square and has one double tangent, not the two distinct
   lines asserted by the source solution.
4. The multiplicity definition says that a general point is moved to the
   origin by a linear change. The displayed shift is an affine translation;
   use that correct term and disclose the source wording.
5. Reconcile the `Tangent_to_a_curve.svg` attribution conflict exactly as in
   the component-rights section above.
6. Add one visibly labeled scope note retaining the squarefree plane-curve
   premise around the derivative criterion and declining an unconditional
   schemes-level generalization. This is edition guidance, not source prose.
