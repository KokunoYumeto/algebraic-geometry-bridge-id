# O016/D100 corpus rationale — controlling checkpoint

Date: 2026-08-22  
Scope: settled corpus architecture and production boundary

## Disposition

**GO for the two-volume Brenner design.** Complete *Algebraische Kurven* plus
complete *Bündel, Garben und Kohomologie* (BGK), with a concentrated 19-unit
BGK route and a finite original mastery/integration layer, is the strongest
lawful, buildable, self-study-oriented design for O016/D100. This conclusion
does not rest on sunk translation work.

## Exact boundary

1. Translate all 30 lectures and all 30 worksheets of Holger Brenner's
   *Algebraische Kurven (Osnabrück 2025–2026)* in source order, including every
   frozen public solution and component-rights closure. Its semantic
   Wikiversity/transclusion closure is textual authority; HTML, generated
   LaTeX, and the official 337-page PDF are independent build witnesses.
2. Translate all 30 lectures and all 30 worksheets of Brenner's *Bündel,
   Garben und Kohomologie (Osnabrück 2019–2020)* in source order, again with
   every lawfully frozen public solution and component-rights closure.
3. Expose a separate 19-unit learner route through BGK Units 2–15 and 23–27,
   joined by an independently written terminology/prerequisite seam. Add three
   completely worked mastery items per route unit, 12 solved integrative
   problems, and one solved Stacks-navigation capstone with an oral-proof
   rubric. Preserve source-edition unit identity; the route never renumbers it.
4. Keep Napkin Part XX as optional reference evidence only, and Stacks as a
   downstream permanent-tag reference only.

## Why this combination is strongest

Brenner's classical volume supplies a coherent course rather than isolated
notes: a fixed 30-lecture/30-worksheet sequence, substantial exercise density,
public solutions where the course exposes them, and an unusually rich editable
closure (semantic wiki source, rendered HTML, generated LaTeX, and PDF
witnesses). BGK supplies the missing scheme-theoretic sequence in the same
authorial, terminological, licensing, and semantic-source ecosystem: gluing,
sheaves, ringed spaces, `Spec`, schemes and morphisms, dimension, `Proj`,
quasi-coherent and locally free sheaves, differentials, derived functors,
sheaf/Čech/projective cohomology, Euler characteristic, genus, and
Riemann–Roch. Both are CC BY-SA 4.0 and have complete wiki/TeX/HTML/PDF reader
surfaces. Together they close every named D100 outcome without forcing an
encyclopedic reference to function as a textbook.

## Serious alternatives and why they lose here

- **Napkin Part XX:** compact, readable, lawful, and editable, but its six
  active files stop at affine schemes and morphisms; it supplies neither a full
  general gluing sequence nor cohomology. Its whole-repository build and media
  rights are also less self-contained. Retain it as optional explanation.
- **Stacks Project:** the correct permanent technical reference, with source
  and stable tags, but far too large and reference-shaped to be first assigned
  coursework. Use it for guided navigation and downstream verification.
- **MIT OCW 18.726 (Spring 2009):** broad graduate coverage and 12 problem sets
  under CC BY-NC-SA, but no complete editable mathematical source/build closure
  and no solutions. It is weaker for maintenance and independent study.
- **Andreas Gathmann, *Algebraic Geometry*:** pedagogically excellent and
  compact, but its official surface exposes a rights-reserved PDF rather than a
  lawful editable derivative basis.

## Remaining gaps and exact remedy

Neither Brenner volume exposes public solutions for every worksheet problem;
unexposed source solutions must not be invented. The selected BGK route has 13
observed public solutions for 314 exercise occurrences, so the original layer
must reach three worked items per unit: currently 44 new unit-level solutions,
plus 12 fully solved integrative problems and the capstone. Some repetition of
affine varieties, ideals, `Spec`, and Zariski language is intentional
transition scaffolding. The complete two-volume library is 602 pages; the
concentrated learner route is 494 source pages before original connective and
mastery material.
