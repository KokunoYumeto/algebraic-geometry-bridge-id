## Primary Indonesian evidence

The following Indonesian academic sources independently use `proyektif` for
projective geometry and projective space:

1. Silvia Oktarini, Haripamyu, and Shelvi Ekariani, "Ruang Proyektif Kompleks
   sebagai Manifold Kompleks Berdimensi 1", *Jurnal Matematika UNAND* 7(2),
   2018, Universitas Andalas:
   https://jmua.fmipa.unand.ac.id/index.php/jmua/article/download/326/317
2. Denik Agustito, Irham Taufiq, and Muhammad Irfan, "Ruang Proyektif
   Kompleks CP^n sebagai Ruang Faktor S^(2n+1)/S^1", *Teorema: Teori dan
   Riset Matematika* 7(1), 2022, DOI:
   https://doi.org/10.25157/teorema.v7i1.6709
3. Ganeswara Pramudita, "Bijeksi yang mempertahankan garis proyektif pada
   ruang proyektif real Pn", undergraduate thesis, Universitas Indonesia,
   2020:
   https://lib.ui.ac.id/detail?id=20514490&lokasi=lokal

The sources cover the same construction used here: projective space as a
quotient of nonzero vectors, homogeneous coordinates, affine charts, and the
real or complex topological model. They are therefore directly relevant term
witnesses rather than general-language analogies.

## Decision and propagation

Use `proyektif` throughout human-facing Unit 27 text. In particular, admit
`ruang proyektif`, `garis proyektif`, `bidang proyektif`, `himpunan titik nol
proyektif`, `varietas proyektif`, and `subruang linear proyektif`.
`Projektif` may remain only as a recorded search variant or inside exact German
source identifiers; it is not the preferred Indonesian geometry spelling.
